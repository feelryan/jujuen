**行動 (Action):**
- 我優化了 Jenkins 與 GitLab 流程，並引入 AI 輔助開發工具（如自動生成單元測試與初步 Code Review）。｜I optimized Jenkins and GitLab workflows and introduced AI-assisted development tools (e.g., for auto-generating unit tests and preliminary code reviews).

**結果 (Result):**
成功減少了人工審查時間，提升了交付速度（Velocity），這也呼應了我最近獲得的 GenAI Leader 認證的實踐。｜Successfully reduced manual review time and increased delivery velocity, aligning with the practices from my recent GenAI Leader certification.

## 4. 常見追問與應對 | Likely Follow‑ups & Responses

**Q1: Tell me about a time you disagreed with a stakeholder. | 請分享一次你與利害關係人意見不合的經驗。**
- **Angle:** Focus on "Data over Opinion" and "Customer First."
- **Response:** "在 Innova 設計 API 時，PM 希望快速上線但忽略了某個合規性檢查。我沒有直接拒絕，而是列出了潛在的 FDA 審計風險與長期維護成本。最後我們達成共識，分階段實施：先上線核心功能，但保留合規性接口。"｜"When designing APIs at Innova, a PM wanted a quick launch, skipping a compliance check. Instead of refusing, I outlined the FDA audit risks and long-term maintenance costs. We agreed on a phased approach: launch core features first but keep the compliance interface ready."

**Q2: How do you handle a team member not pulling their weight? | 你如何處理團隊成員表現不佳的情況？**
- **Angle:** Emphasize "Empathy" and "Coaching" (Leadership).
- **Response:** "我會先私下溝通，了解是否有阻礙（如技術債或個人困難）。例如在導入 CI/CD 時，有成員抗拒新工具。我發現是因為他不熟悉 Docker，於是我安排了 Pair Programming 環節協助他上手，而非指責。"｜"I communicate privately first to understand blockers (tech debt or personal issues). For example, when introducing CI/CD, a member resisted. I realized he wasn't familiar with Docker, so I scheduled pair programming sessions to help him upskill instead of blaming him."

**Q3: Describe a time you made a mistake. | 請描述一次你犯錯的經驗。**
- **Angle:** Focus on "Post-Mortem culture" (Blameless) and "Systematic Fix."
- **Response:** "在 Hiveel 時，我曾因索引設定錯誤導致搜尋效能下降。發現後我立即回滾（Rollback），修復後不僅修正了程式碼，還在測試環境增加了自動化的效能回歸測試（Regression Test），防止未來再發生。"｜"At Hiveel, I caused a search performance drop due to incorrect indexing. I rolled back immediately upon discovery. After fixing it, I didn't just patch the code; I added automated performance regression tests in the staging environment to prevent recurrence."

## 5. 常見陷阱與糾正 | Pitfalls & Corrections

- **陷阱 1：過度強調技術細節，忽略「人」的因素。｜Pitfall 1: Overemphasizing technical details, ignoring the 'people' factor.**
    - *Bad:* "I used Kubernetes and Istio to fix the latency."
    - *Correction:* "I identified that latency was causing user churn. I led the team to adopt Kubernetes, which required mentoring two junior engineers on microservices patterns..." (加入商業價值與領導力｜Add business value and leadership).
- **陷阱 2：使用 "We" 太多，聽不出你的貢獻。｜Pitfall 2: Using "We" too much, obscuring your contribution.**
    - *Bad:* "We built the medical imaging platform."
    - *Correction:* "While the team built the platform, **I specifically designed** the GCP storage automation and **drove** the CI/CD adoption..." (明確指出你的動作｜Specify your actions).
- **陷阱 3：故事太舊。｜Pitfall 3: Stories are too old.**
    - *Correction:* 盡量優先使用 Innova Solutions (Current) 的例子。Chunghwa Telecom 的例子雖然強大（國家級專案），但僅在需要強調「大規模系統設計」或「硬體整合」時使用。｜Prioritize Innova Solutions examples. Use Chunghwa Telecom stories only when emphasizing "Large Scale System Design" or "Hardware Integration" as they are older.
