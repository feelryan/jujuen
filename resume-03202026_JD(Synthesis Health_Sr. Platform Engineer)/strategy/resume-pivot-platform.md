# 履歷重構：從應用開發轉向平台工程 / Resume Pivot: From App Dev to Platform Engineering

## Why this matters｜為什麼這個主題重要

**1. The "Platform" vs. "Product" Mindset Gap**
Your resume currently reads like a strong **Senior Product Engineer** (focused on features, UI, and business logic like "UI Localization" or "Payment integrations"). However, Synthesis Health is hiring a **Sr. Platform Engineer**. They care less about *what* features you built, and more about *how* you built the underlying infrastructure that enables those features.
您的履歷目前讀起來像是一位資深的 **產品工程師**（側重於功能、UI 和業務邏輯，如「UI 在地化」或「支付整合」）。然而，Synthesis Health 正在招聘 **資深平台工程師**。他們比較不關心您開發了什麼功能，而更關心您如何建構底層基礎設施來支撐這些功能。

**2. Leveraging the "GCP Architect" Certification**
You are a **Google Certified Professional Cloud Architect**, but your resume bullets don't fully prove this skill in practice. The JD specifically prefers GCP. If you don't pivot your resume to highlight architectural decisions, cloud infrastructure, and reliability, you are wasting your strongest credential.
您擁有 **Google 認證專業雲端架構師** 證照，但履歷中的 bullet points 尚未充分證明這些技能的實戰應用。該 JD 特別偏好 GCP。如果您不將履歷重心轉向架構決策、雲端基礎設施與可靠性，就等於浪費了您最強的憑證。

**3. The "Internal Customer" Focus**
Platform engineers build for *other engineers*, not just end-users. The JD mentions "enabling our product teams." You need to show that you improve **Developer Experience (DevEx)** and system stability, not just user satisfaction.
平台工程師的服務對象是 **其他工程師**，而不僅僅是終端用戶。JD 提到「賦能我們的產品團隊」。您需要展現您如何提升 **開發者體驗 (DevEx)** 與系統穩定性，而不僅是提升用戶滿意度。

## Step‑by‑step strategy｜具體行動步驟

### Week 1: Resume Refactoring (履歷重構)

1.  **Reorder Skills & Summary (調整技能與簡介順序):**
    *   Move **Cloud/DevOps** (GCP, Docker, Jenkins) to the top of your "Core Skills" list.
    *   In your Summary, explicitly mention "Platform Engineering" or "Infrastructure Optimization" alongside your Cloud Architect title.
    *   將 **Cloud/DevOps**（GCP, Docker, Jenkins）移至「核心技能」列表的最上方。
    *   在簡介（Summary）中，除了雲端架構師頭銜外，明確提及「平台工程 (Platform Engineering)」或「基礎設施優化 (Infrastructure Optimization)」。

2.  **Rewrite "Innova Solutions" Bullets (重寫 Innova 經歷):**
    *   **Remove/Minimize:** "UI Localization," "Frontend interactive components." These are noise for a Platform role.
    *   **Amplify:** "GCP Storage Bucket Auto-Creation." This is automation/platform work. Expand on *how* it was architected (e.g., using Cloud Functions? Terraform? APIs?).
    *   **Amplify:** "Code quality standards and led technical reviews." Frame this as "Standardizing Engineering Practices" and "Mentorship," which matches the JD's request for leadership.
    *   **刪除/縮減：**「UI 在地化」、「前端互動元件」。這對平台職位來說是雜訊。
    *   **放大：**「GCP Storage Bucket 自動建立」。這是自動化/平台工作。請展開說明架構方式（例如：使用了 Cloud Functions？Terraform？APIs？）。
    *   **放大：**「程式碼品質標準與技術審查」。將其包裝為「標準化工程實踐」與「導師制度」，這完全符合 JD 對領導力的要求。

3.  **Inject Platform Keywords (置入平台關鍵字):**
    *   Add keywords from the JD: **Scalability, Reliability, Observability, Microservices, Distributed Systems, CI/CD**.
    *   加入 JD 中的關鍵字：**擴展性 (Scalability)、可靠性 (Reliability)、可觀測性 (Observability)、微服務 (Microservices)、分散式系統 (Distributed Systems)、CI/CD**。

### Week 2: Narrative Preparation (故事準備)

1.  **Prepare the "Architecture" Story (準備架構故事):**
    *   Select one project (e.g., the Medical Imaging Platform) and practice explaining the **system design**. Not what it does, but how data flows, how it scales, and where the bottlenecks were.
    *   選擇一個專案（例如醫療影像平台），練習解釋其 **系統設計**。重點不是它有什麼功能，而是資料如何流動、如何擴展，以及瓶頸在哪裡。

2.  **Map "App Dev" to "Platform Impact" (將應用開發對應到平台影響力):**
    *   *Old thought:* "I fixed a slow SQL query."
    *   *New thought:* "I improved system throughput and reduced database latency by optimizing query execution plans, ensuring 99.9% uptime."
    *   *舊思維：*「我修復了一個很慢的 SQL 查詢。」
    *   *新思維：*「我透過優化查詢執行計畫，提升了系統吞吐量並降低資料庫延遲，確保了 99.9% 的正常運行時間。」

    > （透過定義 **CI/CD 品質閘門** 與程式碼標準來提升工程卓越性。指導初階工程師關於 **微服務最佳實踐** 與 **雲端原生模式**，直接提升了團隊開發速度並降低生產環境 Bug 率。）

## Common pitfalls｜常見錯誤與避免方式

1.  **Listing Frontend Frameworks too prominently.**
    *   *Mistake:* Spending 20% of the resume talking about React, Redux, or CSS.
    *   *Fix:* Mention them only in the "Skills" section to show you are "T-shaped," but remove them from the experience descriptions unless it's about "Optimizing Frontend Build Pipelines."
    *   **過度強調前端框架。**
    *   *錯誤：* 花 20% 的篇幅談論 React、Redux 或 CSS。
    *   *修正：* 僅在「技能」區塊提及以顯示您是「T 型人才」，但在經歷描述中刪除它們，除非是關於「優化前端建置流程」。

2.  **Using "Maintained" instead of "Scaled" or "Engineered."**
    *   *Mistake:* "Maintained a cloud-native medical imaging platform." (Sounds passive).
    *   *Fix:* "Operated and Scaled a cloud-native..." or "Ensured 99.9% availability for..."
    *   **使用「維護」而非「擴展」或「工程化」。**
    *   *錯誤：* 「維護雲端原生醫療影像平台。」（聽起來很被動）。
    *   *修正：* 「營運並擴展雲端原生...」或「確保...具備 99.9% 的可用性」。

3.  **Ignoring the "Why."**
    *   *Mistake:* "Used Docker and Jenkins."
    *   *Fix:* "Implemented containerization with Docker to ensure environment consistency across dev/prod."
    *   **忽略「為什麼」。**
    *   *錯誤：* 「使用 Docker 和 Jenkins。」
    *   *修正：* 「利用 Docker 實作容器化，以確保開發/生產環境的一致性。」
