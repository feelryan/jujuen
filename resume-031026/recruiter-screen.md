## 3. 關鍵故事與成就 | Key Stories & Achievements

從履歷中挑選 3 個最強的亮點，用來回答「你做過最有挑戰性的專案是什麼？」
Select 3 top highlights from the resume to answer "What's the most challenging project you've worked on?"

### 故事一：醫療影像平台的雲端自動化 (Innova Solutions)
### Story 1: Cloud Automation for Medical Imaging Platform
- **情境 (Situation)**: 醫療影像平台需要為新客戶快速建立隔離且合規的儲存環境。
  - **Situation**: The medical imaging platform needed to rapidly create isolated, compliant storage environments for new customers.
- **任務 (Task)**: 設計自動化流程以減少手動配置錯誤並符合 FDA 規範。
  - **Task**: Design an automated process to reduce manual configuration errors and meet FDA regulations.
- **行動 (Action)**: 開發了 GCP Storage Bucket 自動建立機制，並實作了針對不同地區（英國/愛爾蘭/加拿大）的 UI 本地化與合規設定。
  - **Action**: Developed a GCP Storage Bucket Auto-Creation mechanism and implemented UI localization and compliance settings for different regions (UK/Ireland/Canada).
- **結果 (Result)**: 提升了客戶導入速度，並確保了 FDA 510K 產品區域設計規範的合規性。
  - **Result**: Accelerated customer onboarding and ensured compliance with FDA 510K Product Area Design Specifications.

### 故事二：電商搜尋引擎優化 (USTOSHOP / Hiveel)
### Story 2: E-commerce Search Engine Optimization
- **情境 (Situation)**: 舊有系統在處理百萬級商品搜尋時效能不佳，且缺乏模糊搜尋功能。
  - **Situation**: The legacy system performed poorly with million-level product searches and lacked fuzzy search capabilities.
- **行動 (Action)**: 導入 ElasticSearch 進行全文檢索與多欄位索引，並使用 Java Spring Boot 重構後端 API。
  - **Action**: Implemented ElasticSearch for full-text and multi-field indexing, and refactored backend APIs using Java Spring Boot.
- **結果 (Result)**: 實現了即時且精準的搜尋結果（包含鄰近搜尋），顯著提升了後端效能與使用者體驗。
  - **Result**: Achieved real-time, accurate search results (including proximity queries), significantly improving backend performance and UX.

### 故事三：國家級警報系統 (Chunghwa Telecom)
### Story 3: National Emergency Alert System
- **情境 (Situation)**: 需要一個高可靠性的系統來發送國家級緊急訊息（如地震、災防）。
  - **Situation**: Needed a highly reliable system to broadcast national emergency messages (e.g., earthquakes, disaster prevention).
- **行動 (Action)**: 領導 e-SAV 雲端平台與國家緊急訊息警報平台的架構設計，整合 eGov 系統。
  - **Action**: Led the architecture design for the e-SAV cloud platform and the National Emergency Message Alert Platform, integrating with eGov systems.
- **結果 (Result)**: 成功交付了跨部門的大型系統，確保在緊急狀況下的訊息傳遞穩定性。
  - **Result**: Successfully delivered a large-scale cross-functional system, ensuring message delivery reliability during emergencies.

## 4. 常見追問與應對 | Likely Follow‑ups & Responses

### Q1: 你為什麼想離開目前的公司？
### Q1: Why are you looking to leave your current role?
- **回答角度**: 追求成長、新挑戰、對貴公司特定技術/領域感興趣。
  - **Angle**: Seeking growth, new challenges, interest in the company's specific tech/domain.
- **示範**: "我在 Innova 學習了很多關於醫療合規與 GCP 的知識，但我現在尋求一個能更深入運用我的全端架構能力，並解決更大規模消費者端問題的機會。"
  - **Sample**: "I've learned a lot about healthcare compliance and GCP at Innova, but I'm now looking for an opportunity to apply my full-stack architecture skills more deeply and solve larger-scale consumer-facing problems."

### Q2: 你的履歷上有 Java 也有 Node.js，你更偏好哪一個？
### Q2: Your resume lists both Java and Node.js. Which do you prefer?
- **回答角度**: 強調「工具服務於目標」，展現雙語（Polyglot）優勢。
  - **Angle**: Emphasize "tools serve the goal" and showcase your polyglot advantage.
- **示範**: "我對兩者都很自在。在需要強型別與大型企業架構時，我會選 Java Spring Boot；而在需要快速迭代或處理高 I/O 的微服務時，我喜歡 Node.js/TypeScript。我能根據專案需求靈活切換。"
  - **Sample**: "I'm comfortable with both. For strong typing and large enterprise architectures, I lean towards Java Spring Boot. For rapid iteration or high I/O microservices, I enjoy Node.js/TypeScript. I switch seamlessly based on project needs."

### Q3: 你如何跟上新技術（如 GenAI）？
### Q3: How do you keep up with new tech (like GenAI)?
- **回答角度**: 提到證照與實際應用。
  - **Angle**: Mention certifications and practical application.
- **示範**: "我最近剛獲得 Google Cloud Architect 與 GenAI Leader 的認證。在工作中，我也主動利用 AI 工具進行程式碼重構，成功縮短了 Code Review 的時間。"
  - **Sample**: "I recently earned my Google Cloud Architect and GenAI Leader certifications. At work, I actively use AI tools for code refactoring, which has successfully reduced code review cycles."

## 5. 常見陷阱與糾正 | Pitfalls & Corrections

- **陷阱 1：花太多時間講 10 年前的電信經驗。**
  - **Trap 1**: Spending too much time on telecom experience from 10 years ago.
  - **糾正**: Recruiter 關注的是你「現在」能做什麼。請將 70% 的時間花在 Innova 和 Hiveel/USTOSHOP 的經驗上（Cloud, React, Node, Java），電信經驗作為「深厚基礎」一句帶過即可。
  - **Correction**: Recruiters care about what you can do *now*. Spend 70% of the time on Innova and Hiveel/USTOSHOP experience (Cloud, React, Node, Java). Mention telecom experience briefly as a "solid foundation."

- **陷阱 2：過於謙虛，未強調「全端」與「架構」能力。**
  - **Trap 2**: Being too modest and not emphasizing "Full-Stack" and "Architecture" skills.
  - **糾正**: 你有 GCP 架構師證照且實際維護過複雜系統。請自信地使用「我設計了...」、「我主導了...」等主動詞彙。
  - **Correction**: You have a GCP Architect certification and maintain complex systems. Confidently use active verbs like "I designed..." or "I led..."

- **陷阱 3：忽略非 CS 學位的背景。**
  - **Trap 3**: Ignoring the non-CS degree background.
  - **糾正**: 雖然你是土木工程碩士，但你有 15 年軟體經驗。如果被問到，強調你的工程數學背景讓你對邏輯與結構有更強的掌握，且你已通過實戰證明了能力。
  - **Correction**: Although you have an MS in Civil Engineering, you have 15 years of SW experience. If asked, emphasize that your engineering background gives you a strong grasp of logic and structure, and you've proven your skills in the field.
