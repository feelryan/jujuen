# 行為面試 / Behavioral (STAR) | Behavioral (STAR)

## 1. 目標與範圍 | Goal & Scope
- 展示您在壓力下解決問題、處理人際衝突以及指導團隊的能力。 | Demonstrate your ability to solve problems under pressure, handle interpersonal conflicts, and mentor the team.
- 利用 STAR 原則（情境、任務、行動、結果）將履歷中的技術專案轉化為引人入勝的故事。 | Use the STAR method (Situation, Task, Action, Result) to transform technical projects from your resume into compelling stories.
- 重點涵蓋：重大線上事故處理（e-SAV/警報系統）、流程改進（CI/CD 與 AI 輔助）、以及跨部門溝通。 | Key focus areas: Handling critical production incidents (e-SAV/Alert Systems), process improvement (CI/CD & AI-assisted), and cross-functional communication.

## 2. 簡短開場稿 | Opening Script

**當面試官問：「請分享你的工作風格」或作為行為面試的過渡： | When asked "Share your working style" or as a transition into behavioral questions:**

- 我是一位擁有超過 15 年經驗的資深軟體工程師，專注於建立高可靠性的雲端原生系統。 | I am a Senior Software Engineer with over 15 years of experience focused on building highly reliable cloud-native systems.
- 我的工作風格是「預防勝於治療」與「數據導向」。 | My working style is "prevention over cure" and "data-driven."
- 在中華電信負責國家級警報系統時，我學會了在極高壓下確保系統穩定；而在 Innova Solutions，我則致力於透過自動化 CI/CD 與 AI 工具來提升團隊效率。 | While leading the National Emergency Alert Platform at Chunghwa Telecom, I learned to ensure stability under extreme pressure; at Innova Solutions, I focus on boosting team efficiency through automated CI/CD and AI tools.
- 我習慣主動發現潛在風險，並透過清晰的架構設計與團隊溝通來解決問題。 | I tend to proactively identify potential risks and solve problems through clear architectural design and team communication.

## 3. 關鍵故事與成就 | Key Stories & Achievements

### 故事一：處理重大事故與高壓專案 (Critical Incidents / High Stakes)
**來源：Chunghwa Telecom - e-SAV & National Emergency Message Alert Platform**

- **Situation (情境):**
  - 負責國家級緊急訊息警報平台與 e-SAV 雲端平台，這是涉及公共安全的關鍵系統，不容許失敗。 | Responsible for the National Emergency Message Alert Platform and e-SAV cloud platform, critical systems involving public safety with zero margin for error.
- **Task (任務):**
  - 需整合複雜的電子化政府 (eGov) 介面，並確保在緊急狀況下訊息能即時、準確地傳遞。 | Needed to integrate complex eGov interfaces and ensure messages were delivered instantly and accurately during emergencies.
- **Action (行動):**
  - 我主導了系統架構設計，制定了高階規格書 (High-level specs)，並擔任跨部門溝通的橋樑。 | I led the system architecture design, produced high-level specs, and acted as the bridge for cross-functional communication.
  - 建立嚴格的介面整合測試標準，確保與外部系統對接時的容錯能力。 | Established strict interface integration testing standards to ensure fault tolerance when connecting with external systems.
- **Result (結果):**
  - 成功交付了高可用性的警報平台，並順利引導跨功能團隊完成部署，確保了國家級訊息傳遞的穩定性。 | Successfully delivered a high-availability alert platform and guided the cross-functional team through deployment, ensuring the stability of national message delivery.

### 故事二：流程改進與指導資淺工程師 (Process Improvement & Mentoring)
**來源：Innova Solutions - CI/CD & AI-assisted Development**

- **Situation (情境):**
  - 團隊在醫療影像平台 (Imaging Share) 的開發過程中，代碼審查 (Code Review) 時間較長，且交付速度有提升空間。 | The team experienced long code review cycles and had room for improvement in delivery velocity while developing the Medical Imaging Share platform.
- **Task (任務):**
  - 目標是減少審查瓶頸，提升整體開發速度 (Velocity)，同時維持 FDA 等級的合規性。 | The goal was to reduce review bottlenecks and increase overall development velocity while maintaining FDA-level compliance.
- **Action (行動):**
  - 推動 CI/CD 流程改進，引入自動化檢查以減少人為錯誤。 | Drove CI/CD process improvements and introduced automated checks to reduce human error.
  - 利用 AI 輔助開發工具進行代碼重構 (Refactoring)，並指導團隊如何利用這些工具來預先優化代碼品質。 | Leveraged AI-assisted development tools for refactoring and mentored the team on how to use these tools to pre-optimize code quality.
- **Result (結果):**
  - 顯著縮短了代碼審查時間，提升了交付速度，並讓團隊更專注於複雜邏輯而非語法瑣事。 | Significantly reduced code review time, increased delivery velocity, and allowed the team to focus on complex logic rather than syntax trivia.

### 故事三：衝突解決與跨部門合作 (Conflict Resolution & Collaboration)
**來源：Hiveel - Vehicle Search Engine & Schema Design**

- **Situation (情境):**
  - 在設計車輛搜尋引擎時，後端資料庫模型 (Schema) 與 UI 設計的請求流程 (Request Flows) 發生潛在衝突。 | While designing the vehicle search engine, there was a potential conflict between the backend database schema and the UI design's request flows.
- **Task (任務):**
  - 需要在不影響後端效能的前提下，滿足前端的使用者體驗需求。 | Needed to meet frontend UX requirements without compromising backend performance.
- **Action (行動):**
  - 我沒有直接拒絕需求，而是繪製了 EER 圖表 (EER diagrams) 並主動與 UI 團隊協作驗證流程。 | Instead of rejecting the request, I modeled EER diagrams and proactively collaborated with the UI team to validate flows.
  - 透過視覺化的溝通，我們共同調整了 API 設計，平衡了查詢效率與介面流暢度。 | Through visual communication, we jointly adjusted the API design, balancing query efficiency with interface fluidity.
- **Result (結果):**
  - 成功優化了後端效能，同時確保前端功能如期上線，避免了後期的重工 (Rework)。 | Successfully optimized backend performance while ensuring frontend features launched on time, avoiding late-stage rework.

## 4. 常見追問與應對 | Likely Follow‑ups & Responses

- **Q: Tell me about a time you disagreed with a manager or tech lead.**
  - **應對角度：** 專注於「技術觀點」而非「個人情緒」。舉例在 Innova 或 Hiveel 時，若遇到架構決策分歧，我會製作 POC (概念驗證) 或數據分析來支持我的論點，最終以團隊利益為重。 | **Angle:** Focus on "technical perspective" not "personal emotion." Example: At Innova or Hiveel, if there was an architectural disagreement, I would create a POC or data analysis to support my argument, ultimately prioritizing the team's benefit.

- **Q: How do you handle a junior engineer who keeps making mistakes?**
  - **應對角度：** 強調「系統性解決」而非「責備」。我會檢查是否是 CI/CD 流程不夠完善（如 Innova 的經驗），或者文檔不足。我會透過 Code Review 給予建設性回饋，並鼓勵 Pair Programming。 | **Angle:** Emphasize "systemic solution" rather than "blame." I would check if the CI/CD process is lacking (like my Innova experience) or if documentation is insufficient. I provide constructive feedback through Code Reviews and encourage Pair Programming.

- **Q: Describe a time you failed to meet a deadline.**
  - **應對角度：** 誠實面對。例如在早期專案中低估了第三方整合（如 eGov 或 Payment API）的複雜度。補救措施是：及早通報風險、削減非核心功能 (Scope cutting)、並在事後檢討 (Post-mortem) 中改進估時模型。 | **Angle:** Be honest. For example, underestimating the complexity of 3rd-party integration (like eGov or Payment APIs) in an early project. Remedy: Flagged risks early, cut non-core scope, and improved estimation models in the post-mortem.

*雖然這是行為面試，但針對資深工程師，面試官常會針對故事中的技術細節進行深挖：*

- **System Design:** 在 Chunghwa 的 e-SAV 專案中，你如何設計高可用性 (HA)？如果警報發送失敗，你的重試機制 (Retry Mechanism) 是什麼？
- **DevOps/CI/CD:** 在 Innova 你具體引入了哪些 AI 工具？如何確保 AI 生成的代碼符合 FDA 的合規性與安全性要求？
- **Database:** 在 Hiveel 或 USTOSHOP 使用 ElasticSearch 時，你是如何處理索引 (Indexing) 延遲與資料一致性 (Consistency) 的問題？

## 6. 常見陷阱與糾正 | Pitfalls & Corrections

- **陷阱 1：過度使用 "We" 而非 "I" | Trap 1: Overusing "We" instead of "I"**
  - **糾正：** 面試官雇用的是你。在講述 Chunghwa 或 Innova 的團隊專案時，明確指出「我負責設計了...」、「我主導了...」、「我撰寫了...」。 | **Correction:** They are hiring *you*. When discussing team projects at Chunghwa or Innova, clearly state "I designed...", "I led...", "I wrote...".
- **陷阱 2：故事缺乏衝突或挑戰 | Trap 2: Stories lacking conflict or challenge**
  - **糾正：** 不要只講「一切順利」的專案。強調 e-SAV 的高壓環境或 Innova 的合規性挑戰，這才能展現資深工程師的價值。 | **Correction:** Don't just tell "smooth sailing" stories. Highlight the high-pressure environment of e-SAV or the compliance challenges at Innova to demonstrate senior-level value.
- **陷阱 3：忽略業務影響力 | Trap 3: Ignoring business impact**
  - **糾正：** 不要只說「重構了代碼」，要加上「減少了 Review 時間」或「提升了交付速度」。 | **Correction:** Don't just say "refactored code," add "reduced review time" or "increased delivery velocity."

I look forward to bringing this experience to your company.
